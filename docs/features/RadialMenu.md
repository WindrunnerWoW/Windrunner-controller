# Radial Menu

Quick menu for opening common character panels and Windrunner tools.

## Opening the Menu

Open the wheel with its configured keybind. **Start** is the default controller binding.

## Menu Options

The wheel provides quick access to:

- Character panel and bags
- Spellbook, talents, and quest log
- World map, friends, guild, and LFG panels (when provided by the client)
- Chat input
- Windrunner settings and spell placement

## Navigation

1. Open the wheel with **Start** or its keybinding.
2. With native controller input, tilt **either stick** toward an option. You
   can also use the D-pad or assigned cursor direction keys to move the highlight.
3. Press **A** (Cross on PlayStation) to choose the highlighted option.
4. Press **B** (Circle on PlayStation), **Start** (Options on PlayStation), or **Escape** to close the wheel.

With a mouse, click an option's icon or label to choose it. Click the **X** above the center portrait
or empty space outside the wheel to close it.
Hold a D-pad direction to move through options repeatedly after a short pause.

Choose **Controller > Radial selection stick > Left / Right / Both** to change
which stick selects actions. **Both** is the default and uses the active stick;
a centered second stick does not clear your selection. The same preference
applies to utility rings and is saved with your profile.

With native controller input, the right stick selects menu items while the wheel
is open. The optional mouse bridge enables right-stick camera and pointer
movement, and lets right-stick click (RS on Xbox, R3 on PlayStation) switch
between those modes; `/controller cursor` does the same from chat. These mode
switches require the mouse bridge. In standard UI menus, RS/R3 switches A/X
between focused actions and pointer clicks while the right stick moves the
pointer. Without native analog input, use the D-pad or assigned cursor direction
keys to move the highlight.
The wheel takes over controller input while it is open, so movement and
gameplay shortcuts resume after it closes.

## Customization

The wheel layout keeps common actions in fixed positions so their locations
stay familiar. Unavailable actions remain visible but dimmed and explain why
they cannot be used. LFG becomes available when the client provides an LFG
panel.

Round icons sit inside a segmented ring. The selected segment and icon border
use your appearance accent; the center shows the action name and any availability
message. Change colors, text size, wheel scale, or animation in **Appearance**.

## Tips

- Great for quickly opening panels without keyboard shortcuts
- Works well with controller navigation
- Click outside the wheel to close it

## Editable utility rings and collections

The quick menu retains its built-in navigation and adds Tools and available
Professions collections. The Gameplay settings include the ring editor. Create
up to twelve named rings, each with up to twelve spell, carried-item,
character action-slot macro, or native system-action entries. The action picker
can add spells, items, macros, and native bindings such as **Game Menu**
(`TOGGLEGAMEMENU`), world map, bags, and character panels; controller-only
custom actions stay out of rings. The Tools collection also has a **Game menu**
entry for quick access. Ring editing supports create, rename, delete, add,
remove and reorder. For native input, optional release activation commits a
deliberate stick/D-pad selection when the held ring button is released. Releasing
in the deadzone without a selection cancels. Keyboard-mapped input explicitly
confirms. Use **X / Next collection** to cycle Tools, Professions and saved rings.

Ring contents are character-specific. Shared exports include supported spells,
carried items, and native system actions, but omit macro entries that reference
action slots. Automatic quest-item population and retail-only entry types are
not provided.
